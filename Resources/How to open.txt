PRUNE
=====

Install
-------
1. Drag Prune to the Applications folder.
2. Open Prune.


First launch
------------
Prune is not registered with Apple, so macOS blocks the
first launch. Allow it once:

  macOS 15 or later
    System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway

  macOS 12, 13 and 14
    Right-click Prune ▸ Open ▸ Open

  Terminal (any version)
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Prune.app


After opening
-------------
Follow the assistant to give Prune Full Disk Access.

The interface follows the language of your Mac (English or Portuguese).

Requirements: macOS 12 or later. Apple silicon and Intel.
